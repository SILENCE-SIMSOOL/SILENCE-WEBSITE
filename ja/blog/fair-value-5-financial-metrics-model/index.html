Estimating Intrinsic Fair Value Using the 5 Foundational Financial Metrics

SILENCE
October 19, 2026

In the short run, equity markets operate as sentiment-driven voting machines; in the long run, they function as precision scales weighing operating earnings power and tangible book equity. Investing on impulse or price trends without fundamental grounding inevitably leads to chasing tops and selling in panics.

This publication discloses the intrinsic valuation architecture deployed within Axion, formulating how PER, PBR, ROE, EPS, and BPS interact within a unified quantitative framework.

1. Defining the 5 Pillar Metrics & Their Structural Cohesion

  1. EPS (Earnings Per Share): $\text{Net Income} / \text{Weighted Common Shares}$. Direct measure of earnings power per share.
  2. BPS (Book Value Per Share): $\text{Total Shareholders' Equity} / \text{Common Shares}$. Tangible net asset liquidation value.
  3. PER (Price-to-Earnings Ratio): $\text{Share Price} / \text{EPS}$. Valuation multiple paid per unit of annual net earnings.
  4. PBR (Price-to-Book Ratio): $\text{Share Price} / \text{BPS}$. Multiple of net balance sheet asset backing.
  5. ROE (Return on Equity): $(\text{EPS} / \text{BPS}) \times 100 = \text{Net Income} / \text{Equity}$. Capital allocation efficiency.

These indicators are bounded by the identity: $\text{PBR} = \text{PER} \times \text{ROE}$. High-ROE companies justify elevated valuation multiples; conversely, a low PER accompanied by sub-5% ROE frequently flags a classic 'Value Trap' rather than an undervalued bargain.

2. The Axion Composite Intrinsic Valuation Equation

Axion blends classical Graham fundamentals with modern Residual Income Modeling (RIM):

$$\text{Fair Value} = \alpha \cdot (\text{BPS} \times \text{Target PBR}) + (1 - \alpha) \cdot (\text{EPS} \times \text{Target PER})$$

  • $\alpha$ (Asset Weight): Calibrated dynamically between $0.40$ and $0.60$ for asset-heavy capital equipment, commercial real estate, and banking, while set to $0.25$ for asset-light software platforms.
  • Target PBR: Formulated through the sustainable growth rate $g = \text{ROE} \times (1 - \text{Payout Ratio})$ as $PBR_{\text{target}} = \frac{\text{ROE} - g}{r - g}$, where $r$ denotes cost of capital.

4. Empirical Valuation Simulation

Evaluating a high-quality semiconductor enterprise:

  • BPS: $50.00 / EPS: $6.00 / ROE: 12.0%
  • Peer Target PBR: 1.30x / Peer Target PER: 12.0x / Asset Weight $\alpha = 0.40$

$$\text{Asset Component} = $50.00 \times 1.30 = $65.00$$ $$\text{Earnings Component} = $6.00 \times 12.00 = $72.00$$ $$\text{Fair Value} = (0.40 \times $65.00) + (0.60 \times $72.00) = $26.00 + $43.20 = \mathbf{$69.20}$$

If the market quotes $52.00, the stock reflects an attractive 24.8% discount to intrinsic fair value.
