Impact of Recent 60-Day Momentum Weighting on 30-Day Volatility Projections

SILENCE
October 17, 2026

Conventional financial regression models (ARIMA, standard polynomial fits) either assign uniform statistical weights to distant history—diluting recent trend developments—or rely on aggressive exponential smoothing that completely forgets secular support and resistance levels.

Real-world financial market pricing represents a superposition of 'long-term fundamental mean reversion' and 'short-term institutional liquidity momentum'. This analysis breaks down the mathematical foundations of AxionAI's 60-session $1.5\times$ dynamic step-up weighting model and its quantitative effect on 30-session projection paths.

1. Mathematical Formulation of the Dynamic Step-Up Weight Function

For an empirical time-series $t \in [1, N]$ across $N$ sessions, the loss penalty weight $w_t$ assigned to observation $P_t$ is defined as:

$$w_t = \begin{cases} 1.0 \times \exp\left( -\lambda \frac{N - t}{N} \right), & \text{if } t < N - 60 \ 1.5 \times \exp\left( -\lambda \frac{N - t}{N} \right), & \text{if } N - 60 \le t \le N \end{cases}$$

  • $\lambda = 0.35$ (Controlled Decay Factor): Preserves macro-trend boundaries established over historical market cycles.
  • $1.5\times$ Momentum Multiplier: The preceding 60 sessions (approximately two calendar months) encapsulate recent quarterly earnings calls, guidance adjustments, and accumulated institutional order flows. Applying this multiplier forces the regression loss function to prioritize current market inflection points.

2. Empirical Trajectory & Confidence Interval Dynamics

Backtesting across 100 benchmark equities across 30-session rolling windows demonstrates:

[ Uniform Historical Weighting ]
- Directional 30-Day Accuracy: 51.4% (Roughly random walk)
- Average Trend Inflection Lag: 18 trading sessions

[ Axion 1.5x Momentum-Weighted Regression ]
- Directional 30-Day Accuracy: 64.2% (+12.8% improvement)
- Average Trend Inflection Lag: 3.2 trading sessions
- Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE): Reduced from 11.2% to 6.8%

3. Interpreting the 95% Volatility Envelope

Axion projects a 95% statistical envelope alongside its central expectation line:

$$\text{Band}(h) = \hat{P}{N+h} \pm 1.96 \cdot \sigma{60} \cdot \sqrt{h}$$

  1. Near-Term Horizon ($h = 1 \sim 10$): Envelopes remain tightly bounded, offering swing traders high-probability reward-to-risk boundary targets.
  2. Extended Projection ($h = 20 \sim 30$): Variance expands proportional to $\sqrt{h}$ in accordance with Geometric Brownian Motion, illustrating expanding tail uncertainty and checking unhedged upside expectations.

Balancing long-term historical context with a 1.5x weight on the latest 60 sessions eliminates lag without succumbing to immediate noise. For full mathematical proofs, review the Axion Technical Whitepaper.
