Client Privacy Policy

Companion privacy policy for SkyShards client and ad-free web usage.

This tool is provided by SILENCE as an ad-free Hypixel SkyBlock Shard Fusion Calculator and is not affiliated with Hypixel or Mojang. We value your privacy and keep data local and minimal.

What We Collect

  • No personal information is collected from browsing or using the calculators, recipes, shard tables, or fusion line graph.
  • Ad-free & tracker-free: This build removes all third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking tags, and analytics scripts.
  • No account or login is required to use any calculator or client integration route on this site.

Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

All preferences, shard rates, inventory counts, owned attribute levels, and custom prices are stored strictly inside your browser via localStorage. They remain on your device and are never uploaded to third-party servers.

Third-Party Services

  • Live Bazaar pricing fetches public market data directly from api.hypixel.net without personal identifiers.
  • Links to Discord, GitHub, and Silence Hub are provided for convenience and are governed by their respective privacy policies.
