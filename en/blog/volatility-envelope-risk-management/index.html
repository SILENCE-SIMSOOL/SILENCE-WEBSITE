Risk Management & Backtest Verification with Volatility Envelopes

SILENCE
October 16, 2026

Novice investors fixate on single target-price numbers, failing to prepare for endogenous market volatility. Financial markets are probabilistic domains swayed by shifting macroeconomic conditions, liquidity flows, and unforeseen headlines. Managing realistic volatility bands rather than chasing point estimates is vital for long-term survival.

This technical guide outlines the mathematical mechanics behind Axion's 30-session Volatility Envelope and demonstrates how it bounds drawdown risks in multi-year backtests.

1. Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) Diffusion Mechanics

The statistical distribution of asset prices over projection steps $h \in [1, 30]$ follows the diffusion parameters of Geometric Brownian Motion:

$$\text{Upper Band}(h) = \hat{P}{N+h} + 1.96 \cdot \sigma{60} \cdot \sqrt{h}$$ $$\text{Lower Band}(h) = \hat{P}{N+h} - 1.96 \cdot \sigma{60} \cdot \sqrt{h}$$

  • $\sigma_{60}$ (60-Session Historical Volatility): Sample standard deviation of daily log returns over the preceding 60 trading bars.
  • $\sqrt{h}$ Scaling Coefficient: Diffuses variance proportional to the square root of time rather than a linear factor, constructing a reliable 95% Gaussian expectation corridor.

2. Three Algorithmic Position Management Rules

Rule 1: Guard Against Overbought Exhaustion

When price surges rapidly and breaches the upper 95% boundary, the probability of mean-reversion toward baseline regression exceeds 95%. New entries are suspended, and 30% to 50% of open profits are liquidated.

Rule 2: Asymmetric Risk-Reward at Lower Envelope Support

When a fundamentally undervalued equity (screened via Axion Fair Value) tests the lower 95% envelope on receding volume, swing entries are initiated with tight stops (-2% beneath lower boundary). Risk is strictly limited to 2%, while reversion targets offer +15% to +20% upside.

Rule 3: Post-Squeeze Trend Expansion

Prolonged band compression (Bollinger-style squeeze) resolving via high-volume breakout above the upper envelope indicates high-velocity trend initiation.
